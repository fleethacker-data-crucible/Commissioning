# Shellback: intent

Shellback is for a supervisor who has to staff a mission and see where the team is thin, and for the employees whose skills are in question: nobody wants a hidden skills score, and a résumé is a claim, not proof. The demo shows a fictional employee turning a résumé into skill suggestions through a real model (Fairlead, key held in Lazarette), confirming two and dismissing one so that only confirmed skills count; then a synthetic team of twenty who chose to share, with coverage by domain shown only for three or more people, an aggregate brief that names no one, and a mission requirement checked against the people who opted in; and finally what leaves the computer (an opt-in peer card, exported only after two confirmations) and the evidence trail for the AI, Fairlead's own request log. All people and data are synthetic, and the demo says what is not built: a live Halyard link and the consent ledger in the interface.
