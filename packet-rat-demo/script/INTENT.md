# PacketRat demo: intent

For people whose important tasking gets buried in email, and for anyone deciding whether to trust a tool that reads their mail. It shows the real PacketRat reading fourteen invented messages, a real model reached only through Fairlead, a queue where every item carries its reason and source, a reply draft, a briefing with checked sources, the rules and dismissal memory, a timed comparison against doing it by hand (one person, one run each, not a study), and a plain list of what was and was not verified live. PacketRat never sends or deletes mail; a person decides.
