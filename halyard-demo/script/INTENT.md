# Halyard demo: intent

Halyard is for product owners and delivery leads who keep a GitHub-hosted backlog in good order. The demo shows it reading a real backlog (a seeded sample, labelled as synthetic), finding the drift (items without acceptance criteria, missing parents, unagreed sizes), drafting fixes with a model that runs live through Fairlead, and holding every change as a proposal that only a person can approve, before anything is written to the configured backlog. It walks the setup from zero (OpenAI key into Lazarette, shared to Fairlead, Halyard's own Fairlead key in Credential Manager), the architecture at a glance, one review-to-approval-to-undo scenario, the Semaphore export and Outlook draft, and ends on the write audit as the evidence trail. The planned pieces (live Shellback link, a runner on the team's repositories) are labelled as planned.
