# SnapShackle: intent

SnapShackle is for support engineers, analysts and trainers on a locked-down Windows workstation who share screenshots in tickets and reports and must not leak a customer's email, phone number or token while doing it. The demo follows one invented support ticket: capture a purpose-built window, annotate it, let Smart redaction find and cover the email, phone number and token, save it to the local library with an OCR record that shows the values are gone, ask the Fairlead router for alt text and annotation suggestions (live, with SnapShackle's own key), and share it as a file with a hand-off manifest. All content is synthetic and SnapShackle is an expanding prototype. Slack, Teams and Jira upload and a direct Lazarette link are not built, and the hotkey, tray and real-desktop capture paths are not shown. The longer note is in INTENT-v1.md.
