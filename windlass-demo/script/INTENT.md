# Windlass demo: intent

For someone who needs a model far bigger than their PC can hold (deep, batch or overnight work) and for the Fleet tools that ask for it by name through Fairlead. It shows Windlass streaming a GGUF model through memory one layer at a time on an ordinary Windows PC, live: setup from zero, a real streamed reply, the machine's own measured numbers, registration in Fairlead and a real call through the `deep-local` style route, the trust details (Host/Origin 403, queue, locality check) and the evidence trail in both logs. The demo model is a 3B so a viewer can watch it; the 70B timings are quoted from the repo's stress test and labelled as not re-run. Planned and unbuilt items (GPU offload, batching throughput) are said out loud. The earlier "hive mind" concept section was cut from this version; the concept is not built or tested.
